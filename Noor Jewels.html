Before you order

How long does delivery take?

2 to 4 working days in major cities, 4 to 6 elsewhere. Bridal orders should be placed 5 days before your event.

Can I exchange a piece?

Yes, within 7 days if it is unworn and in its box. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number.

How do I care for gold-plated jewellery?

Keep it dry, wear it after perfume, and store it in the pouch it comes in.
