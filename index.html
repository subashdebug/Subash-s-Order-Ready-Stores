How an order works

Three steps, one message.

  1. Customer picks items

    They choose size or option, add to the order and fill in delivery details.

  2. You get one WhatsApp message

    Items, total, name, phone and address arrive together, ready to read and confirm.

  3. You confirm and dispatch

    Reply to confirm. With the Order-Ready Store plan, the order goes into a Google Sheet tracker.
